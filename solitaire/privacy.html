Solitaire Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

This policy covers the Solitaire app for Android (package com.priitv.solitaire), made by Priit Vain, an individual developer.

The short version

Solitaire does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. It has no ads, no analytics, no accounts and does not use the internet.

What stays on your device

To let you carry on where you left off, the app saves on your device:

  • the game in progress (card positions, moves, score and play time)
  • your best score and best time

This data never leaves your device through the app. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

Android backup

If you have turned on Android's backup to your Google account, Android may include this saved game data in your device backup and restore it on a new phone. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings and Google's privacy policy. The developer has no access to it.

Permissions

Solitaire asks for no permissions.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the app ever starts handling data differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
